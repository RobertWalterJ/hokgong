# Vibes and design audit — 20 September 2026

Robert asked for "a contemporary lightweight design… something airy and
wonderful". This is what I found looking at the app cold, and what I changed.

## What it felt like before

**It read as a form, not as a language app.** Every screen was the same object:
a white box with a 1px border and 18px of padding, stacked on a beige field.
Home, question, progress, about — all boxes. Airiness does not come from
rounding the corners of a box; it comes from space, and from not drawing a box
in the first place.

**The Cantonese was not the hero.** On a question the character sat at 2.6rem
inside a card, under a prompt, under a red paragraph of troubleshooting. The one
thing a learner's eye should land on was competing with body copy. In an app
about a language, the language should own the screen.

**The accent was a fill, not an accent.** The Start button was a saturated red
slab 220px wide, and every secondary action was another full-width red bar.
Contemporary lightweight design spends its accent sparingly — a pill, a rule, a
mark — and lets the paper carry the rest.

**Three typefaces were doing two jobs.** Instrument Sans for the interface,
Literata for meanings, Bricolage Grotesque for headings, plus Jyutping in a
fourth style. At 375px that is noise.

**No spacing scale.** Padding 18, margins 16, gaps of 6, 8, 10, 12 and 14 chosen
ad hoc. Rhythm is most of what reads as "designed".

**Honesty had become grey weight.** The app says a great deal that is true and
worth saying — where a gloss came from, what a number does not mean, how to
install a Cantonese voice. Stacked as equal grey paragraphs, that turned the
first run into a wall of troubleshooting before a single word of Cantonese.
The fix is not to say less. It is to tier it: the plain fact in the open, the
detail one tap away.

**Nothing moved, and nothing delighted.** No motion language at all, and the
one good motif in the whole project — the rising pitch contour on the app icon —
appeared nowhere in the app itself.

## What changed

1. **A spacing scale** (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64) and one rhythm applied
   everywhere. Sections breathe; nothing is crowded against a border.
2. **Boxes mostly gone.** Grouping is done with space and a hairline. A filled
   sheet is now reserved for the two places that genuinely need one: an answer
   option, and the example sentence inside a word.
3. **The character is the hero.** 3.6rem, centred, generous tracking, with the
   Jyutping as a quiet caption beneath it in the accent. The question prompt
   shrank to a small uppercase label above it.
4. **The accent got its restraint back.** One filled control per screen — the
   primary action — as a compact pill. Everything else is a hairline pill or a
   plain text button.
5. **Two typefaces.** Instrument Sans carries the interface and the display
   sizes; Literata carries meanings, quotations and the reading voice. Bricolage
   is gone.
6. **Tiered honesty.** The plain sentence stays in the open; the explanation
   moves into a disclosure the learner can open. Nothing was deleted — the "what
   these numbers are not" section is intact, it is simply folded.
7. **The contour motif arrives.** The rising tone-2 line from the icon now sits
   in the wordmark, marks the tone rows, and draws the pitch you actually sang.
8. **A motion language**: 160ms, ease-out, on question changes and reveals, with
   every animation disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## What did not change, deliberately

- **Nothing about the honesty itself.** Every source line, every caveat, every
  "this is a machine voice" note is still there. Folding is not hiding: a
  disclosure is one tap and it is labelled.
- **The dyslexia settings.** Larger text, read-aloud, no timers, generous line
  height, 375px-first layout. Those are constraints, not style.
- **The colour rules.** The palette still passes the build's colour audit — 72
  checks over light, dark-by-phone and dark-by-choice, under deuteranopia,
  protanopia, tritanopia and no colour at all. Right stays blue rather than
  green for exactly that reason.
